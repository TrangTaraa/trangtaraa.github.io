Privacy Policy – Trang Tara Comments

Last updated: 6 November 2026

"Trang Tara Comments" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @trangtara and the Facebook Page "Trang Tara" to read and reply to comments posted on their own videos and posts.

  • What it accesses: public comments on the owner's own YouTube videos and Facebook Page posts (comment text, commenter display name, time), and the titles/captions of those posts.
  • How it is used: only to write and post replies under those comments on behalf of the channel owner.
  • Storage: comment IDs and the replies sent are kept in a private log on the owner's server so the same comment is not answered twice. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Third parties: comment text may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic) solely to draft the reply.
  • Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Deletion: the log is kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate replies.
